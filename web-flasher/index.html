Target: cyd2usb (Dual-USB ST7789)
  1. Connect your CYD to your computer with a data-capable USB cable.
  2. Click Install cydOS and pick the USB serial port (CP210x / CH340).
  3. If connection stalls, hold BOOT while clicking Install until flashing starts.
  4. After reboot, hold BOOT during reset anytime to recalibrate touch.
